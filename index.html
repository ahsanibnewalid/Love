A note from the developer

Humans aren't boolean.

⌘

In the original program, love was a pair of yes-or-no answers. In real life, feelings are more interesting than that.

This playful project grows from that original idea: use code to ask a human question, not to decide someone else's feelings. Respect, consent, kindness, and honest communication matter more than any result on a screen.
